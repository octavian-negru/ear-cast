EarCast is a personal sound-amplification app for Android. Use your phone or a compatible headset microphone to listen through headphones, adjust sound per ear and save sound profiles.

All features are free, including DPDFNet8 processing and Strong quiet-speech boost. EarCast is open source under GPL-3.0, with no purchases or ads.

Features:
* An uncalibrated pure-tone hearing check, with results shown per ear, or manual entry of results from a professional hearing test
* Saved sound profiles, per-ear amplification, dynamic-range compression and live volume controls
* Phone or compatible headset microphone input; Bluetooth performance and latency depend on your hardware
* Listening presets and quick-settings controls
* Optional media sound adjustments for other apps, where supported by the device
* A relative output limiter, comfort controls, instant Stop and automatic stop when headphones disconnect
* Large controls, high-contrast appearance and scalable text
* On-device audio processing without an EarCast account or analytics
* Share your hearing-check results when you choose

Microphone access is used for live assist. An ongoing notification lets you stop assist while using other apps or with the screen off. Live microphone audio is processed in memory and is not saved. Sound profiles stay on your device; you can choose to share hearing-check results.

IMPORTANT: EarCast is not a medical device or certified hearing aid. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Hearing checks are uncalibrated estimates and are not a substitute for professional testing or care. Consult an audiologist or doctor for hearing concerns. EarCast cannot measure sound pressure at your ear or guarantee safe or comfortable listening. Start quietly, keep the volume comfortable, and stop if sound is too loud.

EarCast is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. Hardware compatibility and audio quality vary by phone and headset.
