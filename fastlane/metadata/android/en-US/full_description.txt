EarCast is a private hearing-assistance app for Android. It runs a
pure-tone hearing check, builds a personalized amplification profile from the
result, and uses it to amplify quiet sound — like speech — in real time, with any
headset or earbuds.

Features:
* Pure-tone hearing check (per ear, per frequency) with an adaptive staircase,
  shown as a chart — or enter results from a professional hearing test manually
* A personalized sound profile per ear; save several and switch between them
* Real-time hearing assist, per ear: multi-band gain, dynamic-range compression,
  a feedback/howl guard, and live volume control while it runs
* Environment presets (standard / conversation / outdoors) and a quick-settings
  tile for one-tap on/off
* Experimental: apply your sound profile to music and videos from other apps
* A hard output-loudness limiter and a comfort calibration so sound never gets
  uncomfortably loud, plus an always-available instant stop; assist stops
  automatically if your headphones disconnect
* Accessibility-first: large controls, high-contrast theme, scalable text
* Completely private: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network access. Audio
  is processed on-device and never recorded or transmitted.

IMPORTANT: EarCast is a sound-amplification and hearing-assistance tool. It is
NOT a medical device, NOT a certified hearing aid, and NOT a substitute for a
professional hearing exam. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you
have concerns about your hearing, please see an audiologist or doctor. Keep the
volume comfortable and stop if anything is too loud.
